= Orange
:tags: fruit, citrus

An orange is an edible xref:fruit.adoc[fruit] of various
xref:citrus.adoc[citrus] species in the family Rutaceae.  It primarily refers
to _Citrus × sinensis_, the sweet orange, as distinct from the related bitter
orange.

Unlike the xref:apple.adoc[apple], which is a pome, the orange divides into
juicy segments inside an aromatic rind.
