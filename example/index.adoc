= Example Zettelkasten
:tags: home

This is an example Zettelkasten to demonstrate Luhmann.  It is a small web of
linked notes about fruit: a couple of concrete notes
(xref:apple.adoc[apple], xref:orange.adoc[orange]) and the concepts that
connect them (xref:fruit.adoc[fruit], xref:pome.adoc[pome],
xref:citrus.adoc[citrus]).

Open any note and look at its *Backlinks* section, or the
link:/graph[graph], to see how the notes connect.  Notes are organised by
their `:tags:`, not by folders — everything here lives in one directory.
