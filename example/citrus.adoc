= Citrus
:tags: concept, botany

Citrus is a group of flowering trees and shrubs in the rue family, and the
xref:fruit.adoc[fruit] they bear.  Citrus fruits are known for their juicy
segments and aromatic rind.

The xref:orange.adoc[orange] is a citrus fruit; lemons, limes and grapefruit
are citrus too.
