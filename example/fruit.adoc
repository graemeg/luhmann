= Fruit
:tags: concept, botany

In botany, a fruit is the seed-bearing structure of a flowering plant, formed
from the ovary after flowering.  In everyday use the word usually means the
sweet, fleshy kinds we eat.

Fruits are grouped into many types by how they form, including the
xref:pome.adoc[pome] (such as the xref:apple.adoc[apple]) and the
xref:citrus.adoc[citrus] (such as the xref:orange.adoc[orange]).
